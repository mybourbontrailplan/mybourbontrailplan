Common Questions

I found incorrect information on a distillery listing. Can you fix it?
Absolutely, please email us with the distillery name, what's incorrect, and the updated information. We want every listing to be as accurate as possible and we update the site regularly.
I'm a distillery and want to update our listing or be added to the site.
We'd love to hear from you. Email us with your distillery details including tour options, pricing, hours, and booking information. We review all submissions and add distilleries that meet our editorial criteria. Note that we maintain editorial independence, listings and reviews reflect our honest experience.
Do you offer sponsored placements or paid reviews?
No. Our reviews and ratings are based on real visits. We don't accept payment for reviews, rankings, or featured placements. This policy keeps the site trustworthy for the people using it to plan their trips.
Can you help me plan a custom Bourbon Trail itinerary?
Our 3-day itinerary, booking guide, and distillery directory cover most trip planning needs. If you have a specific question those guides don't answer, email us and we'll do our best to help.
I'm interested in advertising or partnership opportunities.
For partnership inquiries, email us with the subject line "Partnership." We're selective about partnerships and only work with brands and services that are genuinely useful to Bourbon Trail visitors.
