The most affordable tour on Louisville's Whiskey Row and the perfect starting point for first-time Bourbon Trail visitors. Immersive history exhibits, a working artisanal still, and a Prohibition-era Speakeasy bar downstairs.

What to Expect

The Evan Williams Bourbon Experience sits right in the middle of Whiskey Row, making it the most convenient stop to pair with Old Forester and Angel's Envy. At $18, it's also the most budget-friendly tour on the Row, and for what you get, it's a solid deal.

The experience is heavy on history, which sets it apart from the production-focused tours at Old Forester and Angel's Envy. You walk through immersive recreations of 1780s Louisville, see a working artisanal still, and end with a guided tasting. It's the most educational start to a Bourbon Trail trip you can get.

The real bonus is the Speakeasy bar downstairs, a Prohibition-themed cocktail lounge that's worth visiting even without the tour. Excellent craft cocktails at reasonable prices.

How to Book

Evan Williams is one of the easiest bookings on Whiskey Row. Walk-ups are often available, especially on weekdays. A reservation 1–2 weeks out is more than enough for any time slot.

This is a great one to book as your morning warm-up on Day 1, start here at 10 AM, then walk to Old Forester and Angel's Envy in the afternoon.

The Bottom Line

Evan Williams is the best value on Whiskey Row and the ideal first stop for Bourbon Trail beginners. The history-focused approach gives you context that makes every subsequent distillery tour better. The Speakeasy bar downstairs is a hidden gem. Pair this with Old Forester and Angel's Envy for a complete Louisville day.
