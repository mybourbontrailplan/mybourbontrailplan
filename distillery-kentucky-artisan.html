What to Expect

Kentucky Artisan Distillery is best known as the home of Jefferson's Bourbon, including the famous Jefferson's Ocean, which is aged aboard a ship that sails across the globe. The distillery sits in a converted ice cream factory in Crestwood, about 20 miles northeast of Louisville, and the modest exterior belies what's inside. The tours here are genuinely intimate, small groups get a behind-the-scenes look at every aspect of production that larger distilleries can't offer. You'll see the lab where new recipes are developed, watch hands-on bottling, and get close to the equipment in ways that bigger operations don't allow. Tour guides are consistently praised as knowledgeable and entertaining, Bill, Steve, and Laura are names that come up repeatedly in reviews. The Jefferson's Ocean story is the highlight, learning how bourbon is loaded onto a ship, sails through different climates across 30+ ports of call, and how the ocean's rocking and temperature swings affect the aging process. Whether or not you buy the concept, it's one of the most unique bourbon stories on the trail. Kentucky Artisan also does contract distilling and grows their grains on 700 neighboring acres at Waldeck Farm, making them one of the few true farm-to-bottle operations. Note: Jefferson's is building a new distillery in Lebanon, KY, but the Crestwood location remains open.

Tour Options

Distillery Tour & Tasting

★ Top Pick

Full behind-the-scenes tour of the craft distillery including production areas, R&D lab, and bottling line. Ends with a tasting of Jefferson's bourbons including the flagship Reserve and the famous Ocean. Small groups mean personal attention.

💰 $15–$20
⏱ 45–60 min
📅 Book 1 week ahead

Barrel Tasting Experience

Premium

A more intimate, seated experience focused on tasting from the barrel and exploring the full Jefferson's portfolio in depth. Perfect for serious bourbon enthusiasts who want to go beyond the standard tour.

💰 $25–$30
⏱ 45–60 min
📅 Book 1–2 weeks ahead

How to Book

Kentucky Artisan is moderately easy to book. The Crestwood location is off the beaten path enough that it doesn't get massive crowds, but the Jefferson's brand draws enthusiasts. Book online a week ahead for weekends. Weekday visits are almost always available.

📅 Booking Details

Booking Method: Website

Hardest Slots: Sat 11 AM–2 PM

Cancellation: 24hr notice

Walk-Up Chance: Moderate

Gift Shop

The gift shop carries Jefferson's bottles including Reserve, Ocean, and sometimes limited releases. They also sell distillery-exclusive products like Billy Goat Strut Whiskey and Whiskey Row Bourbon that you can only find here. Jefferson's branded merchandise is available. Prices are at MSRP, you won't overpay.

The Bottom Line

Kentucky Artisan delivers one of the most personal, educational tours on the entire trail. The Jefferson's Ocean story is captivating, the small-group format means real interaction, and the behind-the-scenes access exceeds what most major distilleries offer. The facility itself isn't glamorous, but the experience more than compensates. A hidden gem between Louisville and Shelbyville.

Practical Info

Address: 6230 Old LaGrange Rd, Crestwood, KY 40014

Hours: Tuesday–Saturday 10 AM – 4 PM, Sunday 12 PM – 4 PM. Closed Monday.

Parking: Free lot on-site.

Kids: Allowed on tours, no tasting under 21.
