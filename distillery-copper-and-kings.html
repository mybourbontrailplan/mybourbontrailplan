Copper & Kings American Brandy Co.

Not bourbon, and that's the point. Louisville's most unique distillery experience features American brandy, a subterranean sonic aging cellar, and a rooftop bar with skyline views.

What to Expect

Copper & Kings breaks the mold on the bourbon trail, because it's not bourbon. This is an American brandy distillery (with some gin and absinthe), and that difference makes it one of the most refreshing stops on an extended trail trip.

The standout feature is their sonic aging cellar: barrels of brandy are aged underground while subwoofers blast music into the rickhouse. The theory is that bass vibrations interact with the aging process. Whether or not you buy the science, walking into a subterranean cellar pulsing with music is a genuinely memorable experience.

The rooftop bar, "The Overlook," offers craft cocktails with views of the Louisville skyline and is a perfect way to end a Butchertown neighborhood visit. The campus has a cool, artsy industrial vibe that appeals to visitors who appreciate creative spaces.

Located in Louisville's Butchertown neighborhood, it's a short drive from Whiskey Row but feels like a completely different world.

How to Book

Copper & Kings is easier to book than the Whiskey Row distilleries. Since it's brandy rather than bourbon, it draws a different crowd and rarely sells out far in advance. Weekend afternoons fill up fastest, especially in summer when the rooftop bar is most appealing.

Cancellation: 24hr notice

Gift Shop

The gift shop has a curated, boutique feel. Their brandy bottles are priced fairly and make unique gifts that stand out from the sea of bourbon bottles you'll accumulate. The gin and absinthe are worth trying too. Merchandise leans artsy and quirky, fitting for the brand.

The Bottom Line

Copper & Kings is the palate cleanser your bourbon trail trip needs. After three days of bourbon, the brandy is refreshing, the sonic aging cellar is unforgettable, and the rooftop bar is one of the best hangout spots in Louisville. Add it to a Louisville day as your third or fourth stop to break up the bourbon monotony.
