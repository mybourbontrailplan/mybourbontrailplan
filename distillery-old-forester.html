What to Expect

Old Forester holds a special place in bourbon history — it was the first bourbon sold exclusively in sealed glass bottles (1870), and the brand never stopped production, even during Prohibition. Their Whiskey Row distillery is a modern showcase that lives up to that legacy.

The building itself is impressive: a multi-story facility where your tour moves vertically through the distilling process. You start at the top with grain and work your way down through fermentation, distillation, and aging. It's one of the most well-structured tours on the trail — educational without being dry, visual without being gimmicky.

The Bottle Your Own experience is the real draw. You hand-fill a bottle from a barrel selected by their master taster, cork it, seal it, and label it yourself. It's a genuinely premium keepsake and the single best "take something unique home" experience on Whiskey Row.

How to Book

Old Forester is moderately competitive. The standard tour usually has availability at 2–3 weeks, but the Bottle Your Own sells out 3–4 weeks ahead on weekends. Weekdays are easier across the board.

If you can only book one premium experience on Whiskey Row, make it the Bottle Your Own here or the tasting at Angel's Envy — both are genuinely worth the premium.

The Bottom Line

Old Forester is the best all-around experience on Whiskey Row. The tour is the most well-designed, the Bottle Your Own experience is genuinely unique, and the bourbon itself is excellent. If you're only doing one premium tour in Louisville, make it this one. Pair with Evan Williams (morning) and Angel's Envy (afternoon) for a complete Day 1.
