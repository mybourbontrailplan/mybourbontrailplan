What to Expect

MB Roland is the definition of craft distilling, a small pot-still operation in rural Pembroke, deep in Western Kentucky. Founded by Paul and Merry Beth (MB) Tomaszewski, the distillery produces their spirits entirely by hand using traditional copper pot stills.

What makes MB Roland special is their "uncut, unfiltered" philosophy. Their bourbon, white dog, and other spirits are produced without chill-filtering or dilution, what comes out of the barrel is what goes in the bottle. This results in robust, full-flavored spirits that taste noticeably different from the precision-engineered products at major distilleries.

The facility is modest, a converted tobacco barn surrounded by Kentucky farmland. Tours are informal, educational, and often led by the owners themselves. You'll learn about pot distilling versus column distilling, why they choose not to filter, and what "craft" means when it's not a marketing buzzword.

Pembroke is in Christian County near Hopkinsville, making MB Roland a natural pairing with Casey Jones for a Western Kentucky craft distillery day.

How to Book

MB Roland almost never has booking issues. The rural Pembroke location keeps crowds nonexistent. Walk-ups are typical, but call ahead to confirm hours, this is a small operation with limited staff.

Gift Shop

Small and straightforward. Their bourbon and moonshine are available at very reasonable prices, this might be the most affordable distillery shop on the entire trail. The unfiltered bourbon makes a unique gift for whiskey enthusiasts who think they've tried everything.

The Bottom Line

MB Roland is the antidote to bourbon trail fatigue. After days of polished corporate tours, walking into a converted tobacco barn and tasting uncut, unfiltered pot-still bourbon is a reset button. The value is exceptional, the education on craft pot distilling is genuine, and the Western Kentucky countryside is beautiful. Pair with Casey Jones for a full day of outlaw-heritage distilling.

Practical Info

Address: 894 Barkers Mill Rd, Pembroke, KY 42266

Hours: Monday–Thursday 10 AM – 6 PM, Friday–Saturday 9 AM – 8 PM, Sunday 1 PM – 6 PM

Parking: Free lot on-site. Rural location, easy parking.

Kids: Allowed. Farm setting is family-friendly. No tasting under 21.
