What to Expect

Garrard County Distilling Co. is one of the newest stops on the Kentucky Bourbon Trail, opening to visitors in 2025. Located in Lancaster, about 35 minutes south of Lexington, the distillery is part of the expanding bourbon scene in central Kentucky. As a new operation, details on the full visitor experience are still emerging. The distillery focuses on small-batch bourbon production and offers tours and tastings in a welcoming craft setting. Lancaster is a small, historic town worth a brief explore. Best combined with a Lexington distillery day — hit the downtown stops (Fresh Bourbon, James E. Pepper, Barrel House, RD1) then swing south to Garrard County and nearby Wilderness Trail in Danville.

The Bottom Line

Garrard County Distilling is a brand-new trail stop that's still finding its footing. Best for completists and visitors already exploring the Lexington-to-Danville corridor. The new-distillery energy means you might catch the founders' enthusiasm firsthand.
