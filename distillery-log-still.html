Log Still Distillery

One of the best craft additions to a Bardstown-area day. A restored historic distillery campus in New Haven with a relaxed vibe, excellent Monk's Road bourbon, and none of the crowds you'll find at the big names.

What to Expect

Log Still Distillery in New Haven is one of those stops that most first-time Bourbon Trail visitors miss, and then kick themselves for missing. It's a craft distillery operating on a restored historic site about 15 minutes south of Bardstown, with a campus feel that's distinctly different from the polished big-name distilleries.

The vibe here is relaxed, uncrowded, and genuinely craft-focused. You're likely to meet the people actually making the whiskey, and the conversations you have are more personal than anything you'll get at Buffalo Trace or Maker's Mark. Their Monk's Road bourbon is excellent, better than what you'd expect from a smaller operation.

The campus itself is growing, with event spaces and expanded tasting options. It feels like a place that's on the rise but hasn't been "discovered" yet by the mainstream trail crowd. That's part of the appeal. A standout addition is The Amp: their on-site concert and event venue. If your trip timing lines up with a show, it's a uniquely Kentucky experience: live music on a bourbon distillery campus in the countryside. Check their events calendar before you visit.

If you're spending time in Louisville, Log Still also runs Monk's Road Boiler House on Whiskey Row (131 W. Main St.), their fine dining tasting room that pairs Monk's Road pours with an upscale chophouse menu. It's the first distillery tasting room on Whiskey Row to offer full dining, and a solid option for a Louisville evening without making the drive to New Haven.

How to Book

Log Still is one of the easiest bookings in the Bardstown area. Walk-ups are often accommodated, especially on weekdays. A quick reservation a few days ahead guarantees your spot, but you rarely need to plan weeks in advance.

This flexibility is one of the best things about craft distilleries, you can make a game-time decision based on how your Bardstown day is going.

Gift Shop

Smaller gift shop focused on their own products. Monk's Road bourbon is the star, grab a bottle if you enjoyed the tasting, because it's harder to find at retail outside the area. Prices are fair. Don't expect the extensive merchandise of a Buffalo Trace, but that's not what you're here for.

The Bottom Line

Log Still is the quintessential craft distillery discovery, the kind of stop that makes you feel like you found something the crowds missed. Monk's Road bourbon is legitimately good, the vibe is relaxed, and the experience is personal in a way the big names can't be. It's 15 minutes from Bardstown and pairs perfectly with an afternoon after Heaven Hill or Maker's Mark. One of the best additions you can make to a Bardstown day.
