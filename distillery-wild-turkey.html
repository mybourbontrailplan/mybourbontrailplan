Wild Turkey Distillery

Perched on a Kentucky River bluff with dramatic views, Wild Turkey is home to Jimmy Russell, the longest-tenured master distiller in bourbon history. A modern visitor center with solid tours and the Russell's Reserve lineup.

What to Expect

Wild Turkey's distillery sits on one of the most dramatic locations on the entire trail, a bluff overlooking a bend in the Kentucky River, surrounded by old-growth trees. The visitor center is modern and well-designed, opened in 2014, and the views alone are worth the drive.

What makes Wild Turkey special is the Jimmy Russell connection. Jimmy has been the master distiller here since 1954, over 70 years. His son Eddie is now co-master distiller. The Russell family legacy gives Wild Turkey a personal, family-driven feel that's rare at a distillery of this size.

The bourbon itself is bold and unapologetic, higher proof than most competitors, with a rich, spicy profile. If you like your bourbon with backbone, Wild Turkey delivers.

Don't skip the bar. Wild Turkey's on-site bar is one of the best-kept secrets on the trail. Even if you're not doing a full tour, you can stop in for a tasting flight or cocktail with those Kentucky River views. It's a great spot to relax between stops.

Tour Options

Distillery Tour

Solid Option

Standard tour through the production facility with Kentucky River views. Covers the full process and ends with a tasting of the core lineup. Well-run and informative.

💰 $15
⏱ 60 min
📅 Book 1–2 weeks ahead

Russell's Reserve Experience

Top Pick

A deeper dive focused on the Russell's Reserve premium lineup. Smaller groups, more in-depth tasting, and a chance to explore the nuances of their barrel selection process. Worth the upgrade.

💰 $35–$45
⏱ 75–90 min
📅 Book 2–3 weeks ahead

Tasting Only

Quick Stop

If you're short on time or between stops, Wild Turkey offers tasting-only options, no full tour required. Enjoy a guided flight at the bar with views of the Kentucky River. A great way to experience Wild Turkey without committing to a full tour slot.

💰 $10–$20
⏱ 20–30 min
📅 Book Walk-up or same day

How to Book

Wild Turkey is an easy booking. Reservations are recommended, but tours don't face significant sell-out pressure, same-week reservations are usually fine, even for weekends.

Note that Wild Turkey is closed Mondays. Plan accordingly if you're doing a weekday trip.

The Bottom Line

Wild Turkey offers a great combination of stunning natural setting, bourbon history, and bold whiskey. The Russell's Reserve Experience is the standout if you want to go deeper. It pairs naturally with Four Roses and Woodford Reserve on a Lexington/Lawrenceburg day. The Kentucky River views from the visitor center are some of the best on the trail.

Practical Info

Address: 1525 Tyrone Rd, Lawrenceburg, KY 40342

Hours: Tuesday–Saturday 9 AM – 5 PM, Sunday 12 PM – 4 PM. Closed Monday.

Parking: Free, large lot.

Kids: Allowed on tours, no tasting under 21.
