Glenns Creek Distilling

A small craft distillery on the ruins of the legendary Old Crow site in Frankfort, bourbon history nerds will love walking the grounds where some of bourbon's earliest chapters were written.

What to Expect

Glenns Creek Distilling operates on the historic grounds of the Old Crow distillery, one of the most important sites in bourbon history. The ruins of the original limestone buildings are still visible on the property, and walking among them gives you a sense of bourbon's deep roots in this part of Kentucky. Getting out among the ruins is seasonal though, so time your visit accordingly.

The current operation is small and scrappy, a genuine craft distillery that embraces its underdog identity. The owners are passionate about both the history of the site and their own spirits production. Tours here feel more like a conversation than a scripted presentation.

Located just outside Frankfort, Glenns Creek pairs naturally with Buffalo Trace and Castle & Key for a Frankfort-focused day. It's the quietest and most intimate of the three, offering a nice contrast to Buffalo Trace's crowds.

🍂 Come in Fall for the Old Crow Grounds

Glenns Creek does not typically walk visitors around the old Old Crow grounds in summer, because of the heat. If the historic site is the reason you are making the drive, plan for fall. The distillery and the tasting bar are worth a stop year round, but the ruins are the part that goes seasonal.

How to Book

Glenns Creek is almost never sold out. This is a small operation that flies under the radar. Walk-ups are usually fine, but checking ahead for hours is smart since they're a small team.

📅 Booking Details

Gift Shop

Very small, a few bottles and basic merchandise. You're coming here for the history and the craft experience, not the retail therapy. Their OCD (Old Crow Distillery) bourbon is a fun souvenir.

The Bottom Line

Glenns Creek is for bourbon history enthusiasts and craft spirit lovers who want to stand where bourbon history was made. The Old Crow ruins are genuinely fascinating, and the intimate tour is a welcome break from the polished corporate experiences. Pair with Buffalo Trace and Castle & Key for the ultimate Frankfort bourbon day.

Practical Info

Address: 8800 Glenns Creek Rd, Frankfort, KY 40601

Hours: Monday–Saturday 10 AM – 5 PM, Sunday 11 AM – 5 PM

Parking: Free parking on-site.

Kids: Allowed, though the historic ruins require supervision. No tasting under 21.
