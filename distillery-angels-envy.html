What to Expect

Angel's Envy occupies a beautifully restored industrial building on Louisville's Main Street — right in the heart of Whiskey Row. The design is striking: exposed brick, copper accents, and a rooftop bar that overlooks the Louisville skyline. It's the most visually impressive distillery experience in Louisville.

The bourbon itself is distinctive. Angel's Envy finishes their bourbon in port wine barrels, which gives it a sweeter, fruitier profile than traditional bourbon. Whether that's your thing or not, the tasting is a highlight — you'll understand what barrel finishing does to flavor in a way that's hard to learn from reading.

This is also the hardest paid booking on Whiskey Row. Weekend afternoon slots sell out weeks in advance. Book early or plan for a weekday visit.

Tour Options

Distillery Tour

★ Top Pick

The signature experience. Covers the full production process through the beautiful multi-level facility, ending with a guided tasting of their core lineup including the port-finished bourbon. The building itself is worth the visit — some of the best interior design on the entire trail.

💰 $25–$40
⏱ 60–75 min
📅 Book 4–6 weeks ahead

Finishing Touch Tasting

Great Alternative

A seated, deeper tasting experience without the full tour. Smaller group, more focused on the bourbon itself. A great option if the full tour is sold out or if you've already done a couple of production tours that day and want something different.

💰 $35–$50
⏱ 45–60 min
📅 Book 2–3 weeks ahead

How to Book

Angel's Envy is the hardest paid booking in Louisville. The distillery is popular with both bourbon enthusiasts and casual visitors, and the Whiskey Row location makes it easy to add to any downtown itinerary — which means demand is consistently high.

Best strategy: Book 4–6 weeks ahead. If your preferred time is full, try morning slots (10 AM is usually last to fill) or weekdays. The "Finishing Touch" tasting often has more availability than the full tour and is arguably a better experience for serious bourbon drinkers.

Gift Shop

The Angel's Envy gift shop is curated and well-designed — smaller than Buffalo Trace but more focused. Standard bottles are at MSRP. They occasionally have limited releases and single barrel picks available only at the distillery. The branded glassware and merchandise lean premium and make nice gifts.

The rooftop bar is the real post-tour draw. Excellent cocktails with a view. Budget $15–$20 for a cocktail or two — it's worth lingering.

The Bottom Line

Angel's Envy delivers the most polished, design-forward distillery experience in Louisville. The building is stunning, the port-finished bourbon is distinctive, and the rooftop bar is the perfect way to end your Whiskey Row day. The only downside is booking difficulty — plan ahead and you'll be rewarded. Pair this with Old Forester and Evan Williams for a full Day 1.

Practical Info

Address: 500 E Main St, Louisville, KY 40202

Hours: Monday–Saturday 10 AM – 7 PM, Sunday 12 PM – 6 PM (tour times vary)

Parking: Street parking and nearby garages. No dedicated lot — plan for a short walk.

Kids: Allowed on tours, no tasting under 21.
