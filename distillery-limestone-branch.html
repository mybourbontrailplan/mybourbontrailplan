What to Expect

Limestone Branch is one of those places that reminds you why bourbon culture matters. Founded by Steve and Paul Beam, yes, those Beams, this is a seventh-generation distilling family operating a small craft distillery in Lebanon, about 25 minutes south of Bardstown.

The tours are personal and unhurried. Steve Beam himself often leads tours or hangs around the distillery, and the family connection to bourbon history is genuine, not a marketing gimmick. The facility is modest compared to the mega-distilleries, but that's part of the charm.

They produce Yellowstone bourbon (a revived historic brand) and Minor Case rye, along with their own craft offerings. Lebanon is a small town, but the drive from Bardstown is pleasant Kentucky countryside, and pairing Limestone Branch with Maker's Mark (30 minutes away) makes for an excellent day.

How to Book

Limestone Branch rarely sells out. The Lebanon location keeps crowds small, and walk-ups are usually possible. Booking a day ahead is sufficient for weekend visits.

The Bottom Line

Limestone Branch is a must for bourbon history buffs and anyone who wants a personal, family-run experience. The Beam family heritage is real, the tours are intimate, and Lebanon's small-town Kentucky feel is a welcome change from the busier trail stops. Pair with Maker's Mark for a wonderful day south of Bardstown.
